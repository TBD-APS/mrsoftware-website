01 The work

Work slows down where information is moved by hand.

  • Typed twice

    Details are written down in one place and typed in again in another.

  • Spread out

    Messages, documents and files live in different places, so the full picture has to be pieced together.

  • Left waiting

    When nobody can see who has the job now, it waits until someone asks.

05 Our method

Four steps, in this order.

  1. Understand

    We map the work, the bottlenecks and the decision that needs to get easier.

  2. Build

    We develop the smallest solution that can be used in the real process.

  3. Automate

    Repeated steps move into the system, with a person in control.

  4. Measure

    We track quality and activity before we build the next part.

Each part has to work in the real process before we build the next one.
