Behind the software

MR SAAS'y, our shared software foundation.

One foundation, so each application does not build the same things again: identity and access, module entitlements, feature flags and kill switches, audit and release evidence. Applications are meant to use it through versioned contracts, never a shared database.

It started in Workslip. Workslip is live, the shared email-code sign-in was first built for it, and it is the first application registered to move onto the platform.

Results

Minutes to fill in. Days until a person approves.

Two measurements from Workslip. One clock is the time a person spends filling in. The other is the time until a person approves.

The record

3 min

Median active completion time

0 1 2 3

The time a person actively spends filling in. Waiting time is not counted. The bar ends at 3 minutes.

The approval

4.6 days

Median from creation to approval

0 1 2 3 4

The whole elapsed time, including the time spent waiting between steps. The ticks are whole days. The coloured tip runs from day 4 to 4.6.

  • What the minutes show

    The median person spends 3 min filling in. Waiting stays out of that count, so the number is the work on the record.

  • What the days show

    A person still approves the job. The median from creation to that approval is 4.6 days, and the wait is part of the number.

  • What the charts refuse

    Each bar has its own scale. There is no earlier measurement beside them, so the two clocks above are not a saving.

Internal, aggregated measurements from Workslip over a 30-day period, compiled in October 2026. They describe how work flows through the product. There is no before-and-after comparison, so they are not documented time savings, ROI or financial gain for the customer. Ask us how they were measured.
