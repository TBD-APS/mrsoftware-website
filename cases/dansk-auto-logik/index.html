RoadyPilot

The plan stays a suggestion until a person accepts it.

Dansk Auto Logik moves vehicles between sites. Roady keeps each transport, its stops and the events that were actually recorded.

A pilot. The sample transports are synthetic. They are not Dansk Auto Logik's live loads, and they are not a measured saving.

The work

Which vehicle takes the load, and how full the trip is.

Planning means choosing a vehicle for each load and seeing the weight and the space on that trip. The pilot can show the plan you have beside a suggestion. A load that cannot be placed is shown as unplanned. The suggestion is not a finished dispatch.

A person decides

Nothing is applied until someone accepts the plan.

Accepting the plan does not send a real transport. Applying it only stores the sample plan.

  1. 01

    Open the suggestion

    The current plan and the suggestion are shown together.

  2. 02

    See what is missing

    A load that does not fit stays visible, with the reason it was not planned.

  3. 03

    Accept the plan

    A person accepts a complete suggestion. A partial one cannot be accepted.

  4. 04

    Apply it in the sample

    A second step stores that accepted plan as the sample. The company's own orders and fleet are still required for live work.

The boundary

What this pilot does not say.

There is no distance, cost or CO₂ figure, and no quote from Dansk Auto Logik. Driver hours, traffic and how a real car transporter is loaded are outside the model.
