TradeyIn development · Demo

The offer is ready before the chat goes quiet.

The buyer does not open a new system to ask for frozen fish. They write in WhatsApp. Tradey is built so that message becomes an offer, and the offer waits for a person before it is sent.

A working day the product is built for. Not a quote from a named customer, and not a measured saving.

The day

From the message to a contract the buyer can accept in the same chat.

A demo scenario with synthetic data: frozen fish sold from Norway to a buyer in Spain. The shape of the day is the product.

  1. 01

    Request

    The buyer asks on WhatsApp. The request lands on that buyer's page.

  2. 02

    Offer

    The draft uses your products, stock and prices. Today's euro rate is shown.

  3. 03

    Approval

    A person checks credit, margin and shelf life. Only then does it go out.

  4. 04

    Contract

    The buyer accepts in the chat. The offer becomes a contract and an order.

  5. 05

    Invoice

    The invoice is made from the order, the way the buyer's country requires.

  6. 06

    Books

    The invoice goes to bookkeeping, and the buyer's credit is updated.

The choice

We went to the chat, instead of asking the trade to move.

Food traders already agree the deal in WhatsApp, email and a spreadsheet beside a heavy system. Tradey is designed around that day. The core is the same for every trader. The flows, the rules and the connections are fitted to one house.

  • A person sends it

    The system prepares the offer. A person decides. Every action is logged.

  • The rate is shown

    Tradey shows the exchange rate and alerts. It does not buy or sell currency.

  • A pilot comes first

    Real use starts with approval on everything. Larger traders can begin with one department.
