ShippyDemo

The shipment is not rebuilt from an inbox.

An independent forwarder books the same kind of road work every week. The confirmation, the packing list, customs and the proof of delivery belong to that shipment. Shippy keeps them there.

A working day the product is built for. Not a quote from a named customer, and not a measured saving.

The day

From the booking to the invoice, on one shipment.

An example with synthetic data: two trailers on a fixed weekly route from Denmark to Italy. The shipment is created once. Customs and the invoice step are not in the demo yet.

  1. 01

    Booking

    The customer books by email or phone. Parties, goods, route and dates are entered once.

  2. 02

    Documents

    The booking confirmation and the packing list are made from the shipment.

  3. 03

    Customs

    The declaration is prepared, and its status stays next to the shipment.

  4. 04

    Transport

    The carrier is booked and the transport document is attached.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Proof of delivery comes back and closes the shipment.

  6. 06

    Invoice

    What accounting needs goes with the delivered shipment.

The choice

We built it for forwarders who want to stay independent.

Transport, accounting and customs are often three systems, and the shipment is pieced together by hand. Shippy sits between them. It reuses the shipping library from Tradey for tracking. The two are separate products with separate data.
