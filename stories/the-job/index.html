The office sees what the field already recorded.

The job is created in the morning. On site, the checklist is completed and the photos are attached. The approval waits for the office. Nothing is typed a second time to make that true.

A working day the product is built for. Not a quote from a named customer, and not a measured saving.

The day

One job, from the van to the sign-off.

Workslip is live. The queue shows each job and where it stands. The record on site is the record the office approves.

  • Field

    The task, the photos and the checks are recorded where the work happens.

  • Office

    The status is visible without calling anyone to ask how it went.

  • Approved

    The documentation is ready when the job is signed off.

The choice

We measure the work, and we say what the numbers are.

On the front page we publish two figures from Workslip over a 30-day period, compiled in October 2026. A person spends a median of 3 min actively filling in. The median from creation to approval is 4.6 days, waiting included. There is no before-and-after, so they are not documented time savings, ROI or financial gain for the customer.
