Openness & plans

Roadmap: what we build and publish

We prefer openness to secrecy. That is why our plans are public here, just like the numbers, the system status and the validation artefact. The roadmap is adjusted as plans change, and it always shows whether something is planned or done.

Now: Q4 2026

Go-live on mrsoftware.dk

Custom domain binding for the apex domain, so the whole site and the status page run directly on mrsoftware.dk.

System status on the real domain

The public /status page moves from the preview URL to the apex domain and pings every service.

Public /ping spec

A description of the shared /ping contract endpoint (JSON, HTTP 200, CORS), so others can verify us and we can verify each other.

Verified numbers as snapshots

The validation artefact's numbers (tests, coverage, gates) are published as timestamped snapshots that can be cross-checked.

Next: first half of 2027

Scheduled uptime checks with alerting

A move from browser-based pings to server-side scheduled checks that actively alert on outages instead of only showing status.

Public SLOs

Service level objectives are defined and followed up on the system status page. We measure ourselves against them.

Open source, decided repo by repo

An assessment of which repos can be opened: documentation first, then the website and health tooling, as consent and governance are in place.

Standard /ping on every new service

Every new service implements the same contract endpoint from day one. No exceptions.

Later: second half of 2027

Open-source core tooling and documentation

Selected parts are released under a permissive licence. The core is assessed component by component: what stands without secrets is opened.

Public roadmap & issue tracker

Plans and tracking become public, and community contributions go through the same gates we use internally.

Ongoing transparency report

A periodic report: metrics, incidents, what we prevented and what we learned. Published, not promised.

Principles of openness

Public by default

Numbers, status and plans are public unless there is a real reason not to be.

Evidence over promises

Every claim points to a check (a probe, a test, a gate) so the reader can verify it.

Known limitations are shared

Coverage gaps and unused modules are listed in the artefact. They are not hidden.

The roadmap is a living document. Follow operations on the system status page and the numbers in how we validate ourselves.
