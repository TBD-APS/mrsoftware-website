foodtrader er skrivebordet, hvor MR software I/S læser og besvarer WhatsApp-beskeder til virksomhedens eget nummer.

English

foodtrader is the WhatsApp desk of MR software I/S, Ceresbyen 66, st. 1., 8000 Aarhus C, Denmark. A person writes to the company's WhatsApp business number. A person at the company reads that message and replies in the same chat. The reply can include up to three buttons or a short list while WhatsApp's 24-hour window is open. The working desk is private and is not a public shop. It does not take payment. Read the privacy policy, the terms, and how to ask for deletion.

Sådan bruges det

En person skriver til virksomhedens WhatsApp-nummer. En person hos MR software læser beskeden og svarer i samme samtale. Svaret kan indeholde op til tre knapper eller en kort liste, mens WhatsApps 24-timers vindue er åbent. Skrivebordet er ikke en offentlig butik og tager ikke imod betaling.
