Roady is the transport workspace for vehicle logistics. It keeps a transport, its saved stops and the events that say whether each one was observed or only planned.

The figure uses synthetic demo data. It is not a customer result, and Roady does not guess a route, an arrival or a live position.

What the read-only demo shows. Nothing here is a measured customer result.

  • Saved stops

    Each stop is a saved place. A stop without coordinates is shown without a map point.

  • Observed or planned

    Every event says whether it was observed or only planned.

  • No guessed route

    Roady does not draw a road route, estimate an arrival or show a live position unless the source supplies them.
