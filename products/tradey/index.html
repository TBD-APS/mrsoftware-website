The problem

The trade happens in chats. The system finds out later.

Food traders buy and sell across countries and currencies, with credit, shelf life and fast prices. Most of it is agreed on WhatsApp, by email and in spreadsheets, next to an ERP that was never built for that pace.

So the same trade is written down three times: in the chat, in a spreadsheet and in the ERP. Tradey puts the trade in one place, starting from the conversation.

Who it is for

Built for mid-size and large food traders.

  • Trading across borders

    Importers, exporters, wholesalers and agents who buy in one country and sell in another, in more than one currency.

  • Fish, frozen and dry goods first

    That is where we start. Fruit and vegetables come later.

  • WhatsApp, email and spreadsheets

    Teams that agree deals in chats and keep the overview in spreadsheets beside a heavy ERP.

Built for each trader

Principles

What we hold to.

  • Designed, not off the rack

    Each system is designed around one trader's workflow. Building with AI is what makes that affordable.

  • Nothing happens without approval

    The system prepares. A person decides. Every action is logged.

  • Rates are shown, never traded

    Tradey shows exchange rates and alerts. It does not buy or sell currency.

  • Guidance, not advice

    The assistant answers from your own data. It does not give legal or financial advice.

How a project runs

Seven steps, with a pilot before anything goes live.

  1. Mapping

    We follow how a trade runs today and find what can be removed.

  2. Data

    Buyers, lots and prices are cleaned up and imported.

  3. Build

    The core is fitted to you, and the WhatsApp flows are built.

  4. Connections

    WhatsApp, bookkeeping, invoicing and exchange rates are connected.

  5. Pilot

    The system is used for real, with approval on everything and daily fixes. Larger traders start with one department.

  6. Go-live

    Normal operation, with close follow-up.

  7. Operation

    Ongoing running, support and a monthly review.

Questions

Straight answers.

Can I use Tradey today?

Tradey is in development. You can see it as a demo with synthetic data. Open the demo.

Do we have to replace our ERP?

No. Tradey is built to work next to the systems you have, and we connect to your bookkeeping first.

Is it a chatbot?

No. WhatsApp is where requests and offers are exchanged. The assistant is limited to answering from your own data, and a person approves what is sent.

What does it cost?

We do not publish prices yet. Tell us about your trade and we explain what a system for you involves.

What do you not do?

Off-the-rack software, every ERP function there is, and actions without a person's approval.
