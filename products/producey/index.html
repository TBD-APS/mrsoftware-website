Planned workflow

Receive. Reserve. Prepare. Dispatch.

For factories with finished goods to store and customer orders to fulfil. These are the workflows we plan to build.

  • Know what is available

    Receive finished goods and see stock by product and warehouse location, with reserved quantities shown separately. Optional batch and expiry tracking is planned per product.

  • Prepare the customer order

    Reserve stock, show shortages and record partial picking and packing, with the remaining quantity kept on the order.

  • Follow what leaves

    Record collection or dispatch and trace the stock movement back to its order, with a history of who changed what.
