01 How it works

From the job on site to the approval in the office.

The step names in small type are the ones Workslip shows, in Danish.

On site · the technician

The job is recorded in six steps

  1. 1Job detailsSagsdetaljer

    Customer, work site and the fields the job needs.

  2. 2Installation typesAnlægstyper

    The work that was done. It decides which checkpoints come next.

  3. 3CheckpointsKontrolpunkter

    At least one in each relevant category, or marked as not relevant.

  4. 4TimesheetsTimesedler

    Working time and expenses. A job can have several.

  5. 5CompletionAfslutning

    How the work ended, including when it is not finished.

  6. 6AttestationAttestering

    A last check, then the job goes on for review.

In the office

The office reviews the same record

  • In reviewTil gennemsyn

    Waiting for the office after attestation.

  • ApprovedGodkendt

    The office signs the job off.

  • RejectedAfvist

    The job is not accepted as it stands.

  • ReopenedGenåbnet

    The job is open again for changes.

Afterwards

Documentation and overview

  • Documentation ready

    What the office approved is what was recorded on site.

  • Leader analysisLederanalyse

    Jobs per month: approved, rejected and still open.

Use case examples

From a site visit to an office review.

Examples of the Workslip workflow, not named customer stories or measured savings.

  • Example

    Document a service visit

    A technician records the job details, relevant checkpoints, photos and time on site. The office reviews that same job record.

  • Example

    Review work before approval

    The office checks an attested job and its documentation before approving or rejecting it. The decision stays with the job.

  • Example

    Continue an unfinished job

    The technician records how the visit ended, including unfinished work. A reopened job can be updated before the next review.
