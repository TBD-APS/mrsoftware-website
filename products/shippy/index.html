The problem

Three systems, and the shipment lives in between.

A forwarder runs the transport system, the accounting system and the customs system side by side. The shipment itself lives in email threads, attachments and phone calls between them.

Most of the time goes on paperwork between booking and delivery. Shippy keeps that paperwork with the shipment, and shows who has the next step.

Who it is for

Built for independent forwarders.

  • Independent and owner-led

    Forwarders who want to stay independent and need their systems to hang together.

  • Fixed routes and cross-border

    Road, sea and cross-border work, where the same documents come back every week.

  • Transport, accounting and customs

    Teams where these three are separate systems and the shipment is pieced together by hand.

Do we have to replace our transport system?

No. Shippy is built to sit between the systems you have, not to replace them.

How does it relate to Tradey?

They share the same core for shipments and documents. A food trader and a forwarder see the same shipment from their own side.
